    How it ranks

    Season, budget, crowd

    Season

    A month on a place's best-season list scores full. A month next to it scores half. The pillar's height shows this.

    Budget

    Daily cost per person at your travel style, with two people sharing a room. Places above your budget turn grey.

    Crowd

    Our estimate from school holidays, long weekends and festival dates. Packed places drop down the list and suggest a quieter twin.
