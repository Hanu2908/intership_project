Your travel passport

A planner built around how trips actually get decided.

Most travel sites ask you to remember every place you liked across a dozen open tabs. Rangmarg treats each destination like a passport stamp — collect it once, and it stays in your itinerary until you're ready to send it to a planner.

Why a passport, not a wishlist

Wishlists get abandoned. A passport implies momentum — every stamp is a small commitment toward an actual trip. That framing shaped the whole build: search stays fast and local, adding a stamp is a single tap, and the itinerary you build is exactly what gets sent in your enquiry, with nothing re-typed.
